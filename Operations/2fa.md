# 2FA Configuration

## Setting

Click on your profile icon at the bottom left corner and select `Settings`.

<p align="center">
  <img src="./images/open_settings.png" alt="Open Settings">
</p>

### Enable 2FA

Click to enable 2FA in `2FA verify`

<p align="center">
  <img src="./images/enable_2fa.png" alt="Enable 2FA">
</p>

After that, Click `View QR` to view QR code and scan it with `Google Authenticator` app.

<p align="center">
  <img src="./images/scan_qr.png" alt="Scan QR">
</p>

### Login with 2FA

<p align="center">
  <img src="./images/login.png" alt="Login form with 2FA">
</p>

Enter your 2FA code got from Google Authenticator

<p align="center">
  <img src="./images/2fa.png" alt="Login with 2FA">
</p>
